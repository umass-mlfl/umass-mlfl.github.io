## Bio

Dr. Jacob Schreiber is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Genomics and Computational Biology at UMass Chan Medical School, where he runs the Programmable Genomics Laboratory, and a Visiting Scientist at the Broad Institute.

## Abstract

Recent advances in predictive modeling of genomic modalities have resulted in state-of-the-art performance, but the size and complexity of these models have made them ill-suited for many important tasks, such as designing regulatory DNA with specific properties. In this talk, I will introduce a new predictive model, Cherimoya, which uses an order of magnitude fewer parameters than the closest competitor while still outperforming it. This is made possible through the Cheri Block, a new layer that is a middle ground between the transformer and a dilated convolution. Then, I will describe our DNA design algorithm, Ledidi, that minimally edits template DNA to achieve functional outcomes, and showcase recent design successes.
